The dashboard now shows lease, attempt, and cancellation evidence. Worker detail adds a Session panel from `weft.workers.diagnostics` with the session generation, connection instant, last heartbeat, heartbeat age against a stale threshold, and health. Task ledger detail shows the session lease (`leaseDeadline`), attempt lease (`attemptDeadline`), and absolute execution deadline (`scheduleToCloseDeadline`) as three labeled clocks with exact timestamps, a fenced attempt history from `weft.tasks.get`'s `attempts` ordered by attempt and claim time, with each attempt's deployment, build, artifact digest, and workflow revision, and a cancellation panel that reports worker acknowledgement only from the owning attempt's own cancelled disposition. A cancellation Weft force-settled after its grace period reads "Settled after grace period · no worker acknowledgement", never as acknowledged. The Identity row names an already-qualified activity once, and status colors throughout the UI now use tokens Cinder defines. A `weft.tasks.get` response without a valid `attempts` array, or a worker diagnostics instance without a numeric `sessionGeneration`, now renders as a malformed response. The System route's Health & lease tab now shows `weft.system.lease`'s status, holder, fencing epoch, and lease instants in place of the "Lease status not available" note, and renders a response whose status, mode, holder, and loss reason do not form one of `EngineLeaseHealth`'s shapes as malformed. A `weft.tasks.get` response that is not one coherent branch of its state union, or an attempt whose `executionIdentity` drifts from its published shape, also renders as malformed. Long attempt histories show the newest 25 attempts with a control to load older ones, and task detail judges heartbeat staleness by the same 60-second threshold the Diagnostics tab sends to `weft.tasks.diagnostics`.
